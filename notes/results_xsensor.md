# Cross-Sensor Results (shared 5 classes: Ground/Building/Natural/Pole/Car, coord-only, voxel-level mIoU)

## Table 1 — NoAdapt (source-only) cross-sensor gap
| Train ↓ \ Test → | NPM3D val (Velodyne) | Toronto val (Maverick) |
|---|---|---|
| **NPM3D model** | **0.925** (in-domain) | 0.679 (cross) |
| **Toronto model** | 0.848 (cross) | **0.917** (in-domain) |

Cross-sensor gap: NPM3D→Toronto **−0.246**, Toronto→NPM3D **−0.069** (asymmetric; Maverick→Velodyne transfers better).

## Per-class IoU (cross vs in-domain)
NPM3D→Toronto (cross): Ground .950 / Building .806 / Natural .883 / **Pole .184** / **Car .574**
Toronto→NPM3D (cross): Ground .948 / Building .946 / Natural .800 / **Pole .585** / Car .963

**Finding:** large smooth classes (Ground/Building/Natural) transfer robustly; **structured/thin objects (Pole, Car) degrade most cross-sensor** — consistent with the scan-pattern-fingerprint thesis (these depend on beam/ring sampling).

Generated by `scripts/cross_sensor_eval.py` on existing source-only coord models.

## Table 2 — +B (density augmentation, RandomDropout) effect, shared-5 mIoU
| Model | in-domain | cross-sensor | Δ cross vs NoAdapt |
|---|---|---|---|
| NPM3D (NoAdapt) | 0.925 | 0.679 | — |
| **NPM3D +B** | 0.906 | **0.695** | **+0.016** |
| Toronto (NoAdapt) | 0.917 | 0.848 | — |
| **Toronto +B** | 0.913 | **0.856** | **+0.008** |

**B effect:** cross-sensor **+0.012 avg** while in-domain **−0.012 avg** → classic robustness↔fit tradeoff; the gap shrinks. Per-class: structured objects benefit most — **Pole** Toronto→NPM 0.585→0.668 (+0.083), NPM→Toronto 0.184→0.217 (+0.033). (Car mixed.)

**Honest read:** B alone is a *small but consistent* cross-sensor gain in the right direction. The **bigger lever is A (sensor diversity)** — that's what drove the OS road→Building fix qualitatively. A clean held-out-sensor test of A / A+B needs a **3rd labeled sensor** (SemanticPOSS = Hesai, or SemanticKITTI) → protocol P2, next phase.

Configs: `semseg-pt-v3-{,npm3d-}coord-augB.py`. Models: `exp/{toronto3d,npm3d}-ptv3-coord-augB/`.
